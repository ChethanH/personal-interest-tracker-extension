Privacy & settings

You're in control of your browsing data.

Interest tracking

Record page titles and domains to estimate broad topics. Data stays in this browser profile.

AI topic classification (optional)

When enabled, the extension sends only each new page's title and domain to OpenAI for classification. Page text, full URLs, query strings, and form data are not sent. API usage may incur charges. Your API key is stored in this browser's extension storage and is not encrypted by the extension.

Leave AI disabled or the key blank to use local keyword classification. Never use a key you cannot afford to have exposed.

Excluded websites

Enter a domain such as example.com. Pages on that domain and its subdomains won't be recorded.

Delete browsing activity

Remove all visit records stored by Interest Tracker. This won't delete your browser history.

Privacy note

Tracking is controlled by the switch above. By default, topic classification is local and no browsing data is sent to a server. AI classification is opt-in and sends only the page title and domain to the configured OpenAI API. Topic labels and recommendations can be inaccurate.
